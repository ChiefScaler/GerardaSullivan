If you are in crisis

If you are in immediate danger or need urgent support, please contact your local emergency services immediately.

UK

Emergency services: 999
Samaritans: 116 123 — free, confidential, 24 hours a day
Crisis text line: text SHOUT to 85258

International

Contact your local emergency services or visit findahelpline.com to find a crisis line in your country — available in over 30 countries, free to use.
