What I Offer

Support shaped around
your needs.

Online therapy for individuals and relationships. Sessions are confidential, secure, and tailored to you. You don't have to fit into a box.

I offer online therapy sessions designed to fit your schedule and comfort. Sessions are confidential, secure, and tailored to your individual needs, whether you're seeking support for anxiety, trauma, relationships, or personal growth.

Therapy can support many different challenges, whether recent difficulties or long-standing experiences that feel hard to carry alone. What matters most is that you have a space where your story is heard and taken seriously.

01

Emotional Wellbeing

When anxiety, low mood, or stress have become part of daily life, therapy offers a space to slow down, understand what's driving them, and develop new ways of responding. You don't need to be in crisis to ask for support — feeling stuck, exhausted, or unlike yourself is enough.

Anxiety & worry Low mood & depression Stress & burnout Low self-esteem Confidence
02

Life Challenges & Transitions

Life changes — expected and unexpected — can leave us feeling lost, grieving, or uncertain of who we are. Therapy can help you process difficult experiences and find firmer ground, whether you're facing loss, managing a health condition, or navigating a major life shift.

Grief & loss Bereavement Health-related concerns Long-term conditions Life transitions Career & identity changes Retirement & relocation
03

Trauma & Difficult Experiences

Traumatic experiences — whether from childhood or adulthood — can shape how we see ourselves and others in profound ways. Working in a trauma-informed way, I create a safe and paced space to gently explore these experiences, rebuild a sense of safety, and move toward greater self-trust.

Adverse childhood experiences Domestic abuse & coercive control Sexual abuse Self-harm Suicidal thoughts
04

Patterns & Behaviours

Sometimes we find ourselves repeating patterns we'd like to change — in how we think, how we eat, how we manage substances, or how we relate to others. Therapy offers a compassionate space to understand the roots of these patterns and experiment with new ways forward.

OCD Eating & body image concerns Addictive behaviours Relationship difficulties Unhealthy dynamics
05

Relationship Therapy

Relationship therapy isn't just for couples in crisis — it's for anyone wanting to strengthen understanding, improve communication, or work through patterns that keep causing pain. I work with couples, parent and adult child, siblings, friends, and other important connections. Sessions provide a respectful, structured space for both people to be heard.

Couples therapy Communication difficulties Conflict & disconnection Parent & adult child Rebuilding trust Relational patterns

How Sessions Work

Confidential & safe

Everything shared in therapy is confidential. Sessions take place via a secure, encrypted video platform.

A clear beginning

We start with an initial session and a clear agreement, so you know exactly what to expect.

Your pace, your focus

You choose the pace. Some sessions explore feelings; others include practical tools. We review progress together as we go.

Online & flexible

Sessions are currently offered online only, welcoming clients in the UK and internationally.
